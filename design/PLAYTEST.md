# Playtest look-outs

Things to keep an eye on while playing. Nothing here is owed: it is a
checklist, not a ledger. Tick what you notice, ignore the rest. Newest batch
on top; delete a batch once it has been played.

The 10-16 batches (G50–G59) were played and scored (G60): OSRS 9, the raids
10, the Twins 9, all frozen; the MOBA 3 and out of the pool. Only the FPS
is still open, and its polish is landing from the other agents.

## Batch FPS: Extraction (G63, G64)

`--path . res://scenes/module.tscn -- --module fps_extract`, or Quick play.
- The map: does it feel new each run? Can you tell where you are from the
  tower / mast / water tower / lookout? Is the walk in from the edge dull?
- Guards: loud and slow enough to see first? Too easy? Do they get stuck?
- Loot: too much or too little? Ammo scarce in a fun way, or just frustrating?
- The clock: 5 minutes with the flare at 2:30, right length?
- The 14 variants: does each feel different from its base in hand?
- Parked from the map gauntlet (decide if you care): a campus quad of halls
  round a lawn at the centre; road creases where a road meets a lot's pad;
  the ruins' brick scale.
