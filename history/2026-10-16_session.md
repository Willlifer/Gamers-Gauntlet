**Lessons**
- A probe loop of the shape `while not sim.things.is_empty(): sim.step()` hangs for ever the moment the sim ends, because `step()` returns early once `over != ""` and nothing drains the list. The OSRS "keeps firing through a slam" check stood its player in the centre column, which G57's fist halves both cover; the player died on tick 12 and the probe printed nothing for 15 minutes. Guard every such loop on `over` (or a step cap), and give a mechanics-only check a player who can't die.
- A frozen game is a ruling, not a pause: no "small" changes to OSRS, the raids or the Twins without the author asking. The ledger rows are closed, not parked.
- Pulling a game from the pool is cheap when the registry is one list: `SHELVED` beside `POOL`, lookups over both, the grid and the tile count follow the pool. Nothing else assumed the MOBA was rolled.
