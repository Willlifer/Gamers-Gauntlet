## Lessons worth keeping
- **Geometry winding bit twice.** Inside-out boxes, inward caps and ring
  quads facing down read as "flat dark floor" or "hollow ends" in reviews.
  Build self-tests first; render a close-up when a review doesn't match what you built.
- **Run headless checks in real time.** Frames outrun a fixed tick, so a
  check must wait on time, not frames (the "1 casts Fireball" false fail).
- **Godot compresses imported WAVs.** Set loops in the `.import`
  (`edit/loop_mode=2`), not from byte counts.
- **Pose overrides beat new baked clips** for module animations: Black |
  White's self-test stays green and nothing is re-baked.
- **Black | White's chibi arms are thin.** Gestures need a prop or effect in
  the hand (the Giant's fireball and bomb) to read at range.
- **A raw meter is unfair across classes.** Rate a player against a bot of
  the same class.
