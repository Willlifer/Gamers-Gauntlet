# 2026-10-15 — playtest notes, the tournament look, FPS bullets, Cinderbrand

The author played the 10-14 build and came back with notes:
- The music, the MMO feel and the FPS feel are good.
- The tournament wrapper needs work: they "hate the tournament aesthetic".

They gave two Smash Ultimate references (the main menu and a cup bracket) and a list of changes.

**Shell (G27).**
- Lobby: rebuilt as slanted tiles. The surfaces are shader streaks with a sheen; hovering lifts a tile.
- Quick play: a tile for every game, you against a bot. F10 leaves; in a tournament match, F10 forfeits.
- Creator: a 380×736 card, about 19% of the screen.
- Rolls: `SlotReel` handles them. Games, then first-round players, stop in a cascade, and the last pick eases in over 0.5 s.
- Vote: removed.
- Backdrop: `TourneyBackdrop` circles the rooftops, the Dust hallway or the raid courtyard in its own SubViewport, blurred and graded. The matchup switches to that match's map.
- Bracket: winners on the left, losers on the right, the grand final under a drawn trophy with rays. The plates are off-white with portraits from `TourneyPortraits`. Black | White's BWPortraits ignores the tournament hair colour, so it couldn't be reused.

**FPS (G28, G29).**
- Hitscan is replaced by bullets at 375 m/s, flown from the eye and drawn from the muzzle.
- The bot's shots are bullets too, so its accuracy roll now only picks the aim point.
- `FpsMidModule` puts the duel on `dust_mid`. The bot keeps to the tower half.
- New guard: `tools/fps_probe.gd`.

**Raid (G30).** A forked agent built Cinderbrand in the MMO files only, in parallel with the shell work. PAR was re-measured; the priest dropped from 270 to 233 because cleanses cost it GCDs.
